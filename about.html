    The story
    so far.

    Sound has been a constant in my life for as long as I can remember—probably because I grew up moving between countries, absorbing how music and audio shape culture, mood, and memory. That curiosity about the world still informs how I approach every project.

    I landed in NYC in 2016 by way of D.C., started out as a boom op, and fell hard for the craft of film sound. Since then I've been fortunate to mix on everything from indie films and gritty docs to campaigns for brands like Nike, Netflix, BET, WNBA, NWSL, and L'Oréal—and the work has taken me everywhere from the streets of Accra to the hills of Kentucky to studios in midtown, always with a recorder in hand.

    More recently I've been exploring podcasting and post-audio, bringing that same attention to detail to voice-driven storytelling. If you're building a show, launching a campaign, or working on something that just needs to feel right—let's figure out what we can make together.
