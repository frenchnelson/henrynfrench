About

Sound you
don't notice

I'm Henry French — a production sound mixer who's spent over a decade capturing performances on set. I work across narrative film, episodic television, documentary, and commercial work — anywhere a story needs to be heard.

My approach is technical and intuitive in equal measure. The best set audio doesn't draw attention to itself — it disappears into the performance and pulls you deeper into the world of the film.

I also carry a camera when I'm not carrying a boom. The same instinct that chases a clean take chases a good frame.
